5. FINAL SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The V0.3 evidence establishes that the cryptographic execution environment exhibits measurable temporal variability and that scheduler interactions can occur in qualitatively different regimes.
Across five scheduler traces, direct V-DAP -> kworker transitions range from tens per second to hundreds per second, with A4 reaching 4,984 transitions and a maximum concentration of 553 such transitions within one 10 ms window.
The A4 burst is further characterized by 553 paired transitions with median paired interval of 6 us and maximum of 27 us.
These observations support the bounded statement that empirical investigation of V-DAP revealed and characterized temporal scheduling structures during execution.
